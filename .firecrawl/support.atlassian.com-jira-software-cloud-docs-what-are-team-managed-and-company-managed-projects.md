# What are team-managed and company-managed spaces?

Spaces in Jira can be created as either team-managed or company-managed. The core functionality of these are the same, but there are key differences you should know in order to decide what’s right for your team.

To see whether you’re in a team-managed or company-managedspace, select **More actions** () next to the space's name in the header. Or, next to your space's name in the sidebar, select **More actions** (), then at the bottom of that menu that opens, you’ll be able to view whether your space is:

- Team-managed

- Company-managed


## Team-managed spaces

Set up and maintained by anyone on the team, team-managedspaces are ideal for autonomous teams who want to control their own working processes and practices in a self-contained space. Team-managed spaces have simpler space configuration and give space admins more control over setup without involving a Jira admin (unlike company-managedspaces, where one is required to configure schemes and screens).

Choose a team-managed space if:

- Your team wants easier space configuration to get started quickly.

- You want a self-contained space to manage your team’s work.


## Company-managed spaces

Set up and maintained by Jira admins, company-managedspaces are the best choice for teams who want to work with other teams across many spaces in a standard way, such as sharing a workflow.

Company-managedspaces require a Jira admin to configure screens and schemes that spaces are based on. This means that company-managedspaces have greater complexity in space configuration, but also the ability to standardize workflows across spaces that team-managedspaces don’t have. When a Jira admin changes a scheme or screen, every company-managed space that uses that configuration changes accordingly.

Choose a company-managed space if you:

- want to standardize configuration across multiple spaces in your organization.

- want complex customization for permissions and workflows.
